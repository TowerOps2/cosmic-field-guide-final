The Solar System

Our solar system contains eight planets, dozens of moons, asteroid belts, dwarf planets, and countless mysteries waiting to be explored.

🌍 Earth

Earth is the only known planet to support life and contains vast oceans, diverse ecosystems, and a protective atmosphere.

🪐 Saturn

Saturn is famous for its stunning ring system made of ice, rock, and dust particles orbiting the gas giant.

🔴 Mars

Mars is known as the Red Planet and is one of the primary targets for future human exploration missions.
